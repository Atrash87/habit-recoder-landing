🎯 5 Rules for Habit Success

Science-backed principles to build lasting habits

1
Start Small
Begin with the easiest version — even 2 minutes counts.
💡 Why: Small wins build consistency and confidence
2
Attach It to a Cue
Link your habit to something you already do daily (coffee, waking up, brushing teeth).
💡 Why: Cues make habits automatic
3
Track It Daily
Check it off once per day — yes/no is enough.
💡 Why: Tracking reinforces effort and keeps you aware
4
Never Miss Twice
Life happens. If you miss a day, just get back on track immediately.
💡 Why: Missing once is normal; missing twice starts a pattern
5
Celebrate Progress
Notice streaks and improvements. Even small victories matter.
💡 Why: Rewards strengthen the habit loop
